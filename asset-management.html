How We Work

We work in two modes:

1. Asset Liquidation

You send us the asset list, photos, and technical data. We review remotely and search for a buyer. We only visit your site if a buyer is interested.

2. Asset Assessment

You invite us to your site to prepare an asset list or conduct a full valuation. The visit is at your request, at your expense, and charged by the hour.

Access Requirements: The assets must be accessible and arranged to allow safe inspection. If the site does not allow free movement and easy access, the visit may be rescheduled or the scope of work revised.

If you decide to proceed with the sale, the assessment fee is deducted from our commission.
